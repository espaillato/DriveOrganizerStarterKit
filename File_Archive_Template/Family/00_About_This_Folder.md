# Family

**What goes here:** person-centric documents for non-primary-household people — parents, grandparents, children.

**Placement rule:** if the organizing axis is a person (not your own household's primary adults) rather than a topic, it belongs here — see the Family Override Rule in `Organization_Philosophy.md`: a checkup for a child stays in `Family/[Child]/`, not `Health/`, even though it's a health document.

This does **not** apply to your household's own primary adults — their documents follow the normal topical categories (Health, Finance, etc.).

Common subfolder pattern: one subfolder per person or sub-group (e.g. `[Parent_Side_A]`, `[Parent_Side_B]`, one per child), added as documents accumulate — see Organization_Philosophy.md's Family Subfolders section.

Delete this file once you've filed real documents here, or leave it — it does no harm either way.
